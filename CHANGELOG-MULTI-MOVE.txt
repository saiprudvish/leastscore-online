LeastScore multi-card move fix

- Move supports selecting multiple cards to LEAVE.
- Legal leave groups: 2/4 same rank OR odd same-suit sequence (3/5/7...).
- Choose DECK or OPEN, then press Move.
- The picked card remains in the hand.
- Declaration no longer requires exactly five cards or a special five-card pattern.
- Remaining hand can shrink as cards are left.
- Round scoring uses remaining hand score.
- Bots use the same multi-card leave mechanic.
